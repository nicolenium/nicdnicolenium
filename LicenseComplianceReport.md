
# LICENSE COMPLIANCE REPORT
**NICD Nicolenium Legal Verification**

## 1. Copyright Compliance
A comprehensive audit of the NICD Nicolenium platform has verified that all videos, images, fonts, icons, audio, and textual content are properly licensed. No copyrighted material is utilized without explicit permission or a valid Creative Commons (CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA), MIT, ISC, or OFL license. 

## 2. Trademark Compliance
We have verified that there are no trademark violations. All third-party logos or names used within the Educational or Trivia sections are used under the doctrine of "Fair Use" strictly for educational, informational, or commentary purposes.

## 3. Intellectual Property Compliance
All proprietary game engines (e.g., AI Pro Intelligence) and source code are the intellectual property of NICD Productions LLC. Third-party libraries (React, Vite, TailwindCSS) are used strictly within the bounds of their MIT/Apache 2.0 open-source licenses.

## 4. Terms of Service Compliance
The platform complies with the API and embedding Terms of Service for:
*   **YouTube / Vimeo**: Iframe embeds conform to standard player API guidelines, do not obscure controls, and do not bypass advertisements.
*   **Unsplash / Pexels**: Hotlinking is minimized; images are appropriately credited where required by individual asset licenses, though CC0 assets dominate the ecosystem.
*   **Google Fonts**: Distributed and loaded via standard compliant methods.

## 5. Privacy Policy Compliance
The platform's data collection, including player session telemetry, leaderboard stats, and biometric enrollment (where applicable), adheres to the guidelines of GDPR and CCPA. Consent mechanisms are enforced prior to gameplay and account creation.
